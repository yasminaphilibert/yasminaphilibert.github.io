A campaign for CLŌS CLUB, a members' app for trading clothes rather than buying them.

The whole brand rests on one line — wear it, love it, pass it on — so the campaign never argues the case. It just photographs people who already live that way: a man eating spaghetti on a balcony in a shirt older than the flat, a woman on a rooftop in a jacket someone else broke in first.

One type system throughout, set flush left against the frame, with the accent colour rotating from poster to poster so a wall of them reads as one campaign rather than one poster repeated.

---

Thirty-two of them, sequenced as a magazine you turn by hand.
