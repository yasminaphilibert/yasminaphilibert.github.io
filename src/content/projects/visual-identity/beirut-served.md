A self-initiated concept for a Lebanese canned cocktail line.

Nine flavours, each built from a memory rather than a flavour note — pickles and brine, lazy cake at five o'clock, cedar honey, ghazel el banat. Every can is photographed with the things it is made of, so the ingredient list is the image. Bilingual type sets Latin against Arabic at the same weight, neither translating the other.

Every poster is signed the same way: BEIRUT, SERVED.

---

The series doubles as a controlled comparison. Each layout was resolved twice from identical inputs, and the two results sit on top of each other here — click to turn the page between them.

The differences are not subtle once you look: how much of the editorial column survives, how the light falls across the tray, and how legible the small print stays.
